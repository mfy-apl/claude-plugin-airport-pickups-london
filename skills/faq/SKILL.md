# Airport Pickups London — FAQ & Service Knowledge

## Booking & Confirmation

**How will I know if my booking is confirmed?**
You'll receive an immediate confirmation email with your booking reference, journey details, and a link to Manage Booking. WhatsApp confirmation also sent if available.

**Can I make changes to my booking?**
Yes. Update pickup time, date, addresses, or notes up to 24 hours before pickup via Manage Booking. For same-day changes, contact support 24/7 on 0208 688 7744.

**Can I cancel my booking?**
Yes. 12+ hours before pickup: free (£10 admin fee). 6-12 hours: 50% charge. Under 6 hours: no refund. Cancel through Manage Booking or call 0208 688 7744.

## Driver & Pickup

**Do you provide Meet & Greet?**
Yes, included free. Driver waits inside Arrivals Hall after customs with a name board. Meeting point maps are in the confirmation email.

**Can I see my driver's info before pickup?**
Yes. In Manage Booking: driver name, photo, licence number, vehicle registration, and live GPS location.

**Can I track my taxi in real time?**
Yes. Log into Manage Booking to view live GPS, ETA, and vehicle details.

**I can't find my driver — what do I do?**
Call 0208 688 7744 or WhatsApp +44 7425 987310. Use free airport Wi-Fi if your phone isn't working.

## Pricing & Payment

**Are there any additional charges?**
No. All prices are fixed and include flight monitoring, waiting time, and airport parking.

**Are prices per car or person?**
All prices are per vehicle, not per passenger.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Credit/Debit Cards (Amex, Visa, Mastercard), Cash (GBP), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Revolut, WeChat Pay, AliPay.

**Can I pay in other currencies?**
Drivers require GBP for fares. US Dollars or Euros accepted for tips only.

**Do I need to tip the driver?**
Discretionary. 10-15% appreciated but not mandatory.

## Flights & Delays

**What if my flight is delayed?**
We track flights in real time and adjust pickup automatically — no extra charge.

**What if my flight is cancelled?**
Full refund or rescheduling offered.

**How long will my driver wait?**
30 minutes free at airports, 60 minutes at cruise ports. Extended charges apply after.

## Children & Family

**Child seats?**
Free. Max 3 per vehicle. Rear-facing (0-12mo), forward-facing (1-4yrs), booster (4-12yrs). Customers can bring their own.

**UK law on child seats in taxis?**
Taxis/PHVs are exempt. Children 3+ must wear adult seatbelt. Under-3s may travel unbelted on rear seats. Child seats recommended.

## Special Services

**Wheelchair access?**
WAV vehicles available. Email info@aplcars.com in advance.

**Lady drivers?**
Available on request — mention at booking.

**Pets?**
Guide dogs free. Other pets: £25 valeting charge, must be harnessed.

**Unaccompanied minors?**
Dedicated UMS for under-16s. £50 plus fare. Book via phone only.

**Porter service?**
Available at airports. From £35. Porter meets at baggage carousel (arrivals) or kerbside (departures).

**VIP Meet & Assist?**
Airplane door to vehicle service. Ideal for first-time travellers, families, VIPs.
